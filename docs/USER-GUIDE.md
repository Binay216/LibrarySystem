# Finding What You're Looking For

A quick guide to searching the library's collection of books, music and toys - online from home, or on the touchscreen at the library.

> 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: the library's home page]**

## Searching from home (or anywhere) on the website

You don't need an account or to log in to search - anyone can browse the full collection.

1. On the library's website, click **Search the Catalog** on the home page (or **Catalog** in the menu at the top).

   > 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: the home page with the "Search the Catalog" button highlighted]**

2. Type a word or two into the search box - it matches against the title and the description, so you don't need the exact title. For example, typing "wizard" will find a book described as being about a boy who discovers he's a wizard, even if the word "wizard" isn't in the title.

3. Narrow things down if you like, using the boxes next to the search bar:
   - **Type** - just Books, just Music, or just Toys.
   - **Branch** - only show items currently at one particular library location.
   - **Available only** - tick this to hide anything that's currently on loan to someone else.

4. Click **Search**.

   > 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: a filled-in search form with results showing below it]**

### Reading the results

Each result is shown as a card with everything you need to know at a glance:

- A coloured label in the top corner shows the **type** (Book, Music or Toy) and whether it's currently **Available**, **Borrowed**, **Damaged** or **Destroyed**.
- The **title**, author/artist (or toy type), and a short description.
- At the bottom: the item's code, and which **branch** currently has it - useful if you're planning to visit in person, since an item can move between branches over time.
- If an item isn't available and other people are already waiting for it, you'll see a note like "**3 waiting**" - that's how many people are ahead of you in the queue if you were to join it now.

> 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: a close-up of one result card, with the branch and "waiting" note visible]**

If you want to join the queue for something that's on loan, visit the library and place a hold at the self-service kiosk (below) or ask a staff member.

## Searching at the library (the self-service kiosk)

Each library branch has a touchscreen kiosk you can use without a password or an account - just your library card number when you want to borrow something, check your account, or join a queue.

> 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: the kiosk's idle/welcome screen, showing the branch name and the three big buttons]**

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Search**.
2. Type using the on-screen keyboard - no physical keyboard needed, just tap the letters.

   > 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: the kiosk search screen with the on-screen keyboard]**

3. Tap **Search** to see the results.

The results show the same information as the website - the title, whether it's available, and which branch currently holds it.

> 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: kiosk search results, including one unavailable item with a "Place a Hold" button]**

### Joining the queue at the kiosk

If a result you want isn't available, you'll see a **Place a Hold** button next to it. Tapping it will:

1. Ask for your library card number (using the on-screen number pad), if you haven't already entered it this visit.
2. Confirm the hold and tell you your position in the queue.

> 📷 **[Screenshot placeholder: the kiosk's hold confirmation screen, showing a success message and queue position]**

### A few things worth knowing about the kiosk

- Every screen has a **Cancel / Start Over** button in the top corner - tap it any time to go back to the welcome screen.
- If the kiosk is left untouched for about a minute, it automatically returns to the welcome screen by itself, ready for the next person - so there's no need to worry about leaving your card number visible to whoever uses it after you.
- The kiosk can also be used to check out items you're taking home, and to check your own loans, fines and holds - look for **Check Out** and **My Account** on the welcome screen.
